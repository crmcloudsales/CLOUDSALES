## Deliberate limitations
- No official standalone website was verified during this research.
- No verified public business email was found during this research.
- `Vicente Alberto` appears publicly as an Airbnb host; this is not evidence that the person is NUMA's legal owner, CEO or director, so the public landing does not make that claim.
- The CloudSales page must not describe itself as NUMA's official website unless authorization is separately established.
- Do not copy source prose verbatim beyond short factual labels; all public-facing copy is original/paraphrased.
- Do not store third-party hotel photography in the repository without a verified reuse right. The current landing intentionally uses original CSS artwork instead of scraped hotel photos.
