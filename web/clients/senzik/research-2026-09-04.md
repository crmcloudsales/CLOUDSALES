# Senzik Residences — research dossier

Research date: 2026-09-04

## Scope

Sources reviewed include Senzik's official web properties, Tulum municipal public records, Tulum Country Club material surfaced through Senzik, public listing portals, LinkedIn, and independent due-diligence material. Marketing claims that conflict across sources are not treated as settled facts.

## Project identity

- Senzik Residences is marketed as a boutique luxury residential project inside Tulum Country Club, Riviera Maya.
- The official Senzik website currently attributes copyright to `SNZK RESIDENCES DESARROLLADORES INMOBILIARIOS`.
- A Tulum municipal authorization dated 2025-09-23 identifies the condominium as `SENZIK`, classifies it as a vertical residential condominium, and identifies `ALFEB GRUPO INMOBILIARIO, S. DE R.L. DE C.V.` in the proceeding.
- That authorization states 14 exclusive residential units, 2,898.40 m² total private area, 1,068.12 m² total common area, and a 2,255.73 m² lot.
- The same municipal authorization records a MXN 5,598,515.48 bond in favor of the municipality to guarantee construction quality and latent defects in connection with the Senzik condominium regime.
- A separate Tulum environmental/vegetation-removal authorization names `SNZK RESIDENCES DESARROLLADORES INMOBILIARIOS, S. DE R.L. DE C.V.` and imposes environmental conditions, including a minimum permeable-area requirement and a requirement for environmental authorization/exemption before covered activities.

## Legal/entity distinction

Do not collapse the following names into a single entity without checking the purchase contract and corporate documents:

1. `ALFEB GRUPO INMOBILIARIO, S. DE R.L. DE C.V.` — appears directly in the municipal condominium authorization for Senzik.
2. `SNZK RESIDENCES DESARROLLADORES INMOBILIARIOS, S. DE R.L. DE C.V.` — appears in a municipal environmental/vegetation-removal permit and in the current official-site copyright line.
3. `Tulum Country Club / Grupo Piñero` — the master-community/fraccionador context is separate from the independent developer/seller of Senzik.

For a buyer, the controlling counterparty is the legal entity actually named in the reservation/purchase contract. Verify its corporate deed, authority of signatories, land title/rights, condominium regime, permits, guarantees, and PROFECO registration where applicable.

## Tulum Country Club context

Senzik's own Tulum Country Club pages claim roughly 400 hectares, a 300,000 m² park, 27 golf holes, three controlled access points, and 80+ amenities. A separate Senzik-branded domain has published different figures (including 600+ hectares / 70+ amenities). Because of these inconsistencies, the CloudSales landing avoids repeating disputed total-area/amenity-count claims as hard facts.

## Price and inventory conflicts

Public web sources show materially different historical prices and availability, so no fixed price is presented as current on the CloudSales landing.

Examples:

- `senzik.mx` has historically advertised residences from about MXN 9.5M.
- `web.senzik.mx` has advertised from about MXN 11.3M.
- Third-party listing portals show still other historic asking prices.
- FEUDO's 2026 due-diligence page cites Senzik MXN/USD price lists with PDF metadata dated 2025-09-25. Its snapshot reports 14 units total and, at the date of that source material, 10 sold, 1 reserved and 3 available. This is a dated snapshot, not verified current inventory as of 2026-09-04.

Landing policy: ask the user to request current inventory, current price, unit-specific surfaces, payment schedule and written commercial terms.

## Developer/public-footprint findings

- Publicly indexed results strongly tie ALFEB Grupo Inmobiliario to Senzik.
- Carlos Velez has a public LinkedIn profile associated with ALFEB Grupo Inmobiliario and states 18+ years of experience in purchasing, supply chain and projects.
- In broad public web searches, Senzik is the project that could be corroborated most clearly for ALFEB. No additional ALFEB-branded Riviera Maya development was found with comparable primary-source corroboration in this research pass, so no other project is presented as an ALFEB project without evidence.
- Independent analysis by FEUDO also treats ALFEB as the independent builder/developer behind Senzik and distinguishes it from Grupo Piñero/Tulum Country Club.

The official Senzik site contains dedicated sections for:

These pages support that the project has been presented with both renders and construction-progress/model-unit material. They do not by themselves establish a current completion/delivery date; any delivery date should be verified in the latest signed commercial/legal documents.

## Risk / due-diligence items worth asking for

- Exact seller/developer legal entity in the contract.
- Acta constitutiva and powers of the signing representative.
- Title/ownership or development rights for the project parcel.
- Condominium regime and its current registration status.
- Construction license and current completion/termination-of-work evidence.
- Environmental authorization/exemption required by the municipal permit.
- Copy/validity/status of the construction-quality and latent-defects bond referenced by the municipality.
- Unit-specific plans and final approved surfaces.
- Current price list and current availability, dated the day of quotation.
- Payment schedule, cancellation/default clauses, delivery deadline and remedies for delay.
- PROFECO registration of the adhesion contract where applicable.
- HOA/maintenance fees, Tulum Country Club membership/access costs, golf/beach-club conditions and any separately charged amenities.
