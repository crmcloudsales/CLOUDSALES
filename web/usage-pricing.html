How dynamic billing works

Customer usage price = actual landed provider cost × your plan multiplier

“Landed provider cost” means the real event-specific cost CloudSales incurs to deliver the usage. Depending on the service, this can include provider charges, carrier or pass-through fees, AI inference, TTS/voice-engine costs, telephony legs, verification charges or other unavoidable provider costs tied to that event.

Because these inputs can vary by country, carrier, AI model, channel, provider, marketplace, platform, currency, taxes and transaction structure, two otherwise similar events may have different underlying costs or final prices. CloudSales therefore uses dynamic pricing rather than an artificial one-price-fits-all rate.

No guessed usage charges. When the underlying provider cost required for dynamic billing cannot be reliably determined, CloudSales may delay or reconcile the charge rather than invent a price. CloudSales does not intentionally bill known metered usage below its landed provider cost.

Subscriptions are separate

CloudSales subscription fees pay for access to the platform and the features included in your plan. Metered provider usage is billed separately when applicable. Current subscription pricing is Basic US$47/month, Pro US$97/month and Premium US$147/month. Each CloudSales Member has an individual subscription entitlement, which may be paid directly by the member or sponsored by an organization. Annual pricing and trial terms are displayed at checkout.

CloudSales may route work among supported providers to improve cost, availability or quality. Prices, costs and applicable multipliers can vary when a service is purchased, delivered or billed through a different provider, carrier, marketplace, platform, country, currency, tax regime or technical route. The applicable price is disclosed through the relevant pricing, checkout, marketplace or account experience before the charge when required.
