Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 29, 2026 · Applies globally
This Privacy Policy explains how CloudSales handles personal data across its website, PWA, Cloudy AI operator, AgentCloud agents, CRM and integration features, lead-quality systems, support, billing, and related digital services. The CloudCo operating entity identified on your invoice, order form, subscription record, or applicable contracting document is the relevant service provider and data controller for account-level processing described below.

1. Our roles

CloudSales can process personal data in different roles. For account registration, billing, security, product administration, service analytics, and our own lawful business operations, the applicable CloudCo operating entity generally acts as a controller or business. When a customer uses CloudSales to process its own leads, contacts, customers, employees, prospects, or CRM data, the customer generally acts as controller/business and CloudSales acts as processor/service provider on that customer’s instructions, subject to applicable law and any Data Processing Addendum.

2. Personal data we may collect

Depending on how you use CloudSales, we may process:

  • Account data such as name, business name, email address, phone number, locale, time zone, role, and account identifiers.
  • Subscription and transaction data such as plan, billing status, invoice information, payment references, usage, taxes, promotions, and marketplace purchase information. Full payment-card details are ordinarily handled by our payment provider rather than stored by CloudSales.
  • Business and CRM data such as leads, contacts, opportunities, pipelines, appointments, notes, files, messages, campaign information, conversion events, attribution data, and information synchronized from connected services.
  • Integration data such as provider account identifiers, granted scopes, connection status, OAuth tokens, API credentials, and related metadata. Sensitive credentials are intended to be stored using protected secrets infrastructure and are not displayed back to users in plaintext after storage.
  • Cloudy and AgentCloud data such as prompts, instructions, conversation history, tool requests, approvals, model-routing metadata, outputs, automation jobs, and action logs.
  • Website and device data such as IP address, browser, operating system, device type, timestamps, pages or features used, security events, performance information, cookies or similar technologies where used, and diagnostic logs.
  • Lead-quality and anti-abuse data such as request signals, bot indicators, challenge outcomes, validation results, risk scores, and technical attributes used to protect forms and improve lead quality.
  • Support and communications data you provide when contacting us.

3. How we use personal data

We may use personal data to provide and operate CloudSales; authenticate users; create and manage workspaces; connect third-party systems; execute customer-authorized workflows; route AI requests; provide Cloudy and AgentCloud; process payments and subscriptions; maintain lead-quality and security controls; prevent fraud and abuse; provide support; monitor reliability; diagnose errors; improve product functionality; comply with law; enforce our agreements; and communicate service, security, billing, or product information.

4. Legal bases

Where a legal basis is required, we rely on one or more of the following as applicable: performance of a contract; steps requested before entering a contract; legitimate interests such as security, service improvement, fraud prevention, and B2B operations; consent where required; compliance with legal obligations; and protection of legal rights. A customer using CloudSales remains responsible for establishing the lawful basis for personal data it instructs CloudSales to process on its behalf.

5. AI processing

CloudSales may send relevant prompts, context, or data to configured AI infrastructure or model providers in order to generate responses or perform supported tasks. We aim to minimize unnecessary data and use provider settings and contractual controls where available. Customers should avoid instructing CloudSales to place highly sensitive personal data into AI workflows unless necessary, lawful, and appropriately protected. AI model availability and routing may change over time.

6. Connected platforms

When you connect a CRM, advertising platform, messaging provider, email provider, calendar, payment system, analytics tool, or other service, CloudSales may exchange data with that provider according to the permissions you grant and the actions you request. Each third party processes data under its own privacy terms for activities it independently determines.

7. Service providers and subprocessors

We may use cloud hosting, database, content-delivery, security, payment, communications, analytics, support, AI, and infrastructure providers to operate CloudSales. Depending on configuration, these may include providers such as Cloudflare, Supabase, Stripe, AI-model providers, and the CRM, advertising, messaging, email, or productivity services a customer chooses to connect. We require service providers to process personal data only for authorized purposes and subject to appropriate confidentiality and security obligations where required by law.

8. No sale of personal data

CloudSales does not sell personal data for money. We do not disclose customer CRM data to unrelated third parties for their own independent marketing. Certain privacy laws define “sale,” “sharing,” or targeted advertising broadly; where such laws apply, CloudSales will honor legally required choices for any activity that falls within those definitions.

8A. Real-estate inquiries, matching, and professional referrals

When a person submits information through a real-estate landing page, campaign, property inquiry, development inquiry, investment inquiry, rental request, purchase request, sale request, or property-search workflow, the information may be used to respond to the request, understand stated or reasonably inferred search criteria, identify relevant real-estate options, and perform matching.

Where the applicable notice, consent, customer instruction, request, or other lawful basis permits, the information may be shared with third parties or real-estate professionals that may have relevant properties or options. Those recipients may contact the person in connection with the person's real-estate search or inquiry. This referral or matching use is related to the person's request and is not permission to use the information for unrelated independent marketing. Applicable communication preferences, opt-outs, suppression states, and local legal requirements remain in force.

CloudSales customers using these workflows remain responsible for providing required notices and obtaining any consent or other lawful basis required for collection, matching, referral, disclosure, and follow-up communications.

9. Cookies and similar technologies

CloudSales may use necessary local storage, cookies, session technologies, and similar mechanisms for authentication, security, preferences, checkout, PWA functionality, and service operation. Analytics or marketing technologies, if introduced, will be handled in accordance with applicable consent and opt-out requirements.

10. International data transfers

CloudSales is offered internationally and data may be processed in countries other than the country where you or your contacts are located. Where required, we use appropriate transfer mechanisms such as contractual protections, Standard Contractual Clauses, recognized adequacy mechanisms, or other lawful safeguards. Mandatory local transfer requirements continue to apply.

11. Data retention

We retain account and operational data for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security and auditability, comply with legal and tax requirements, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Customer-controlled CRM content is retained according to the account lifecycle, customer instructions, contractual commitments, technical backup cycles, and applicable law. Security and abuse-prevention logs may be retained for a reasonable period after other content is removed where necessary to protect the service or satisfy legal obligations.

12. Security

We use administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal data, which may include encryption in transit, access controls, secret storage, audit logs, network and application protections, rate limiting, authentication controls, backups, and monitoring. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Customers are responsible for protecting their own credentials, devices, connected accounts, and user permissions.

13. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or obtain a copy of personal data; withdraw consent; request portability; opt out of certain processing; or lodge a complaint with a regulator. These rights may arise under frameworks including the GDPR and UK GDPR, California privacy law, Brazil’s LGPD, Canada’s privacy laws, Mexico’s LFPDPPP, and other applicable laws. Rights are subject to legal exceptions and identity verification.

14. Customer-data requests

If your personal data was entered into CloudSales by one of our business customers, that customer is normally responsible for responding to your request. We may direct you to the customer and will assist the customer as required by applicable data-protection law and our contractual obligations.

15. California and similar U.S. state rights

Where applicable, residents may have rights to know, access, correct, delete, and obtain a portable copy of personal information, and to opt out of covered sale, sharing, or targeted advertising. CloudSales will not discriminate against a person for exercising applicable privacy rights. For customer CRM data, CloudSales acts as a service provider/processor and processes that data for the business purposes instructed by the customer.

16. European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and Switzerland

Individuals in these regions may have rights under applicable data-protection law, including access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, portability, withdrawal of consent, and complaint to a supervisory authority. Where CloudSales acts as controller, the legal bases described above apply. Where CloudSales acts as processor, requests should normally be directed to the relevant customer-controller.

17. Mexico, Brazil, Canada, and other jurisdictions

We honor legally applicable rights and requirements, including ARCO-type rights in Mexico, data-subject rights under Brazil’s LGPD, access and correction rights under Canadian privacy law, and equivalent rights under other applicable regimes. Local mandatory rights are not limited by this Policy.

18. Sensitive data

CloudSales is not designed as a general repository for highly sensitive personal data such as government identifiers, precise medical records, payment-card numbers, biometric templates, or other specially regulated information unless a specific CloudSales feature and written agreement expressly support that processing. Do not upload sensitive data merely because a connected system permits it.

19. Children

CloudSales is a business service and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal data directly from children through account registration. If you believe a child has provided personal data to CloudSales outside a lawful customer-controlled context, contact us through the support channel so the matter can be reviewed.

20. Automated decision support

CloudSales may score, rank, summarize, classify, or recommend actions regarding leads and business records. Unless expressly configured under a customer’s lawful process, CloudSales is not intended to make solely automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects on individuals. Customers are responsible for appropriate human review where required.

21. Data Processing Addendum

Business customers that require a Data Processing Addendum, Standard Contractual Clauses, or equivalent privacy terms may request them through the CloudSales support channel. The applicable DPA, if executed, controls over this Policy to the extent of any conflict concerning processor obligations.

22. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as CloudSales, applicable law, or our processing practices evolve. The current version will be posted at this URL with an updated effective date. Material changes will receive additional notice when required by law.

23. Contact and complaints

Privacy questions, rights requests, or complaints may be submitted through the support channel available inside your CloudSales account or through the contact method displayed on cloudsales.app. We may ask for information necessary to verify identity and jurisdiction before completing a request.
