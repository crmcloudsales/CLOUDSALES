1. Who is responsible for your data

CloudCo is the umbrella brand for the covered products. For CloudSales and other CloudCo services where no different entity is expressly identified, the responsible operating entity is CloudSales Soluciones Comerciales, S.A.S. de C.V. Its business domicile is Boulevard Luis Donaldo Colosio, Av. Tulum, Super Manzana 9, Manzana 1, Lote 3, C.P. 77503, Cancún, México.

If a checkout, order form, invoice, account notice or product-specific agreement identifies another CloudCo operating entity for a particular service or jurisdiction, that entity is responsible for the controller-level processing it determines for that service.

2. Our roles: controller and processor

CloudCo can process personal data in different roles. For account registration, billing, security, fraud prevention, product administration, service analytics, support and our own lawful business operations, the applicable CloudCo entity generally acts as controller/business. When a business customer uses a CloudCo product to process its own leads, contacts, customers, employees, prospects or CRM data, the customer generally acts as controller/business and CloudCo acts as processor/service provider on the customer's instructions, subject to applicable law and any Data Processing Addendum.

3. Personal data we may collect

Depending on the product and how you use it, we may process:

  • Account and identity data: name, business name, email, phone number, locale, time zone, role, account identifiers and authentication-related information.
  • Subscription and transaction data: plan, billing status, invoices, payment references, usage, taxes, promotions, marketplace purchase information and billing events. Full payment-card details are ordinarily handled by a payment provider rather than stored by CloudCo.
  • Business and CRM data: leads, contacts, opportunities, pipelines, appointments, notes, files, messages, campaign data, conversion events, attribution data and information synchronized from connected systems.
  • Integration data: provider account identifiers, granted scopes, connection status, OAuth tokens, API credentials and technical metadata needed to operate authorized integrations. Sensitive credentials are intended to be protected server-side and not displayed back in plaintext after storage.
  • AI and automation data: prompts, instructions, conversation history, tool requests, approvals, outputs, model-routing metadata, automation jobs, action logs and context provided to Cloudy, AgentCloud or other supported AI features.
  • Website, PWA and device data: IP address, browser, operating system, device type, timestamps, pages or features used, session information, security events, performance information, cookies, local storage or similar technologies, and diagnostic logs.
  • Lead-quality, fraud and abuse signals: request patterns, bot indicators, challenge outcomes, validation results, risk or quality scores, attribution signals and technical attributes used to protect services and improve data quality.
  • Support and communications data: messages, recordings where lawfully enabled, attachments and information you provide when contacting us.
  • Referral, affiliate and marketing data: referral codes, attribution, eligible conversions, campaign interactions, communication preferences and related program records.

4. How we use personal data

We may use personal data to provide and operate CloudCo products; authenticate users; create and manage workspaces; connect third-party systems; execute customer-authorized workflows; route AI requests; provide Cloudy and AgentCloud; process payments, subscriptions and usage; maintain security and lead-quality controls; detect and prevent fraud or abuse; provide support; communicate service, security and billing information; measure reliability and product performance; diagnose errors; improve product functionality; enforce agreements; comply with law; protect rights and safety; and perform other purposes disclosed when data is collected.

We do not use customer CRM data for unrelated third-party marketing, and we do not sell customer CRM data for money.

5. Legal bases

Where a legal basis is required, CloudCo relies on one or more of the following as applicable: performance of a contract; steps requested before entering a contract; legitimate interests such as service operation, B2B administration, security, fraud prevention and product improvement; consent where required; compliance with legal obligations; and establishment, exercise or defense of legal claims. A business customer remains responsible for the lawful basis for personal data it instructs CloudCo to process on its behalf.

6. Artificial intelligence processing

CloudCo products may send relevant prompts, context or data to configured AI infrastructure or model providers to generate responses or perform supported tasks. We seek to minimize data sent to providers and use appropriate technical, account and contractual controls where available. AI providers may differ by product, geography, feature, customer configuration, cost, reliability or availability.

Customers should not place highly sensitive or specially regulated personal data into an AI workflow unless the feature is designed for that data, the processing is necessary and lawful, and appropriate contractual and security protections are in place. AI output may be inaccurate, and customers are responsible for human review where required.

7. Automated scoring and decision support

CloudCo products may score, rank, summarize, classify or recommend actions regarding leads and business records. Unless a specific product and customer process is lawfully configured for it, CloudCo services are not intended to make solely automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects on an individual. Customers are responsible for appropriate human review, notices and rights mechanisms where applicable law requires them.

8. Connected platforms

When you connect a CRM, advertising platform, messaging provider, social network, email provider, calendar, storage service, payment system, analytics tool or other service, CloudCo may exchange data with that provider according to the permissions you grant and the actions you request. Each third party processes data under its own privacy terms for activities it independently determines.

9. Service providers and subprocessors

CloudCo may use hosting, database, content-delivery, security, payment, communications, analytics, support, AI, domain and infrastructure providers to operate covered services. Depending on configuration, providers may include Cloudflare, Supabase, Stripe, AI-model providers and the CRM, advertising, messaging, email, productivity or other services a customer chooses to connect. We use contractual, technical and organizational controls appropriate to the role of the provider and applicable law.

For customer data processed as a processor/service provider, a Data Processing Addendum or product-specific subprocessor disclosure may provide additional terms.

10. When we may disclose personal data

We may disclose personal data to authorized service providers and subprocessors; to connected services at your direction; to your organization's authorized users; in a corporate transaction subject to appropriate protections; to professional advisers under confidentiality obligations; to authorities or third parties when legally required; and when reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, security, prevent fraud or enforce agreements. We do not disclose customer CRM data to unrelated third parties for their independent marketing.

11. No sale; U.S. state “sale” and “sharing” concepts

CloudCo does not sell personal data for monetary consideration. Some U.S. state laws define “sale,” “sharing” or targeted advertising more broadly than an ordinary sale. If a covered activity falls within those definitions, CloudCo will provide and honor the opt-out or consent mechanism required by applicable law. Customer CRM data processed on behalf of a business customer is handled as service-provider/processor data under the customer's instructions.

12. Real-estate inquiries, matching and professional referrals

When a person submits information through a real-estate landing page, campaign, property inquiry, development inquiry, investment inquiry, rental request, purchase request, sale request or property-search workflow, the information may be used to respond to the request, understand stated or reasonably inferred criteria, identify relevant options and perform matching.

Where the applicable notice, consent, customer instruction, request or other lawful basis permits, the information may be shared with real-estate professionals or third parties that may have relevant properties or options so they can respond to that inquiry. This does not authorize unrelated independent marketing. Communication preferences, opt-outs, suppression states and local legal requirements continue to apply. CloudCo customers using these workflows remain responsible for required notices and lawful bases for collection, matching, referral, disclosure and follow-up communications.

13. Cookies, local storage and similar technologies

CloudCo may use necessary cookies, local storage, session technologies and similar mechanisms for authentication, security, preferences, checkout, PWA functionality and service operation. Analytics or marketing technologies, when used, are subject to applicable notice, consent and opt-out requirements. Where law requires consent before non-essential technologies are activated, CloudCo will use a consent mechanism appropriate to the covered site or product.

14. Communications preferences

Service, security, legal and billing communications may be necessary to operate an account and may not be optional while the account remains active. Promotional communications can be limited or unsubscribed using the method provided in the message, account settings where available, or the applicable support channel. Opting out of marketing does not prevent transactional or security notices.

15. International data transfers

CloudCo products are offered internationally and data may be processed in countries other than the country where you or your contacts are located. Where required, CloudCo uses recognized transfer mechanisms and safeguards, which may include contractual protections, Standard Contractual Clauses, adequacy mechanisms or other lawful transfer tools. Mandatory local transfer requirements continue to apply.

16. Data retention and deletion

CloudCo retains personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, to provide the service, maintain security and auditability, comply with tax and legal obligations, resolve disputes and enforce agreements. Retention depends on the data category, account lifecycle, customer instructions, contractual commitments, legal limitation periods and technical backup cycles.

When data is deleted from active systems, residual copies may remain temporarily in protected backups until they are overwritten or deleted through normal backup cycles. CloudCo may retain deidentified or aggregated information that no longer reasonably identifies an individual. Security and abuse-prevention logs may be retained after other content is deleted when reasonably necessary to protect the service or satisfy legal obligations.

17. Security and incident response

CloudCo uses administrative, technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal data. Depending on the system, these may include encryption in transit, access controls, protected secret storage, audit logs, network and application protections, rate limiting, authentication controls, backups, monitoring and vendor controls. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

CloudCo investigates security incidents and will notify affected customers, individuals or authorities when notification is required by applicable law or contract. Customers are responsible for protecting their own credentials, devices, connected accounts and user permissions.

18. Your privacy rights

Depending on your location and CloudCo's role, you may have rights to access, know, correct, delete or cancel personal data; restrict or object to processing; request portability; withdraw consent; limit certain uses or disclosures; opt out of covered sale, sharing or targeted advertising; request review of certain automated decisions; or lodge a complaint with a regulator. Rights are subject to applicable legal exceptions, identity verification and CloudCo's role in the processing.

If personal data was placed in a CloudCo product by one of our business customers, that customer is normally the controller responsible for responding. CloudCo may direct you to the customer and will assist the customer as required by law and contract.

A request should identify the person making the request, the account or relationship involved, the right being exercised, the personal data concerned and enough information to locate the relevant records and verify identity. CloudCo will respond within the periods and under the procedures required by applicable law.

22. Brazil, Canada and other jurisdictions

CloudCo honors legally applicable rights and requirements, including data-subject rights under Brazil's LGPD, access and correction rights under Canadian privacy laws and equivalent rights in other jurisdictions. Local mandatory rights are not limited by this Notice.

23. Sensitive and specially regulated data

CloudCo products are not general repositories for highly sensitive personal data such as government identification numbers, detailed medical records, biometric templates, payment-card numbers, precise financial-account credentials or other specially regulated information unless a specific feature and written agreement expressly support that processing. Do not upload sensitive data merely because a connected system technically permits it.

24. Children

CloudCo's covered business services are not directed to children and we do not knowingly seek to collect personal data directly from children through ordinary account registration. If you believe a child has provided personal data outside a lawful customer-controlled context, contact the applicable support channel so the matter can be reviewed and handled as required by law.

Business customers that require a Data Processing Addendum, Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent processor terms may request them through the applicable CloudCo support channel. If an executed DPA or enterprise privacy agreement conflicts with this Notice concerning processor obligations, the executed agreement controls for that customer and processing.

CloudCo may update this Privacy Notice as products, law, providers or processing practices evolve. The current version will be posted at this URL with an updated effective date. Material changes will receive additional notice when required by applicable law. Where consent is legally required for a new purpose, CloudCo will request it rather than relying solely on an updated notice.

Privacy questions, rights requests or complaints may be submitted through the support channel available inside the applicable CloudCo product or through the contact method displayed on cloudsales.app. We may request information reasonably necessary to verify identity, authority, account relationship and jurisdiction before completing a request.
